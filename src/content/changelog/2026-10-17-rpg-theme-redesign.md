Swapped the neon sci-fi look for an RPG one. The site now follows your system color scheme with a dark dungeon theme and a light parchment theme, and a header toggle remembers your choice without a flash on load. Headings use MedievalSharp and HUD labels use Pixelify Sans. Panels and buttons have notched pixel frames drawn in pure CSS. Twenty-two 8x8 icons were drawn by hand for skills, quest tiers and the character sheet, and a new credits page lists every font, library and icon with its license.
