summary: Every job now has its own prop, accent colour and motion on the avatar, so all nine jobs look different.
---

Jobs used to share three props by slot, so a Knight, a Ranger and an Enchanter all carried the same banner. Each of the nine jobs now has its own two-frame prop. Warrior: Knight's crest shield, Paladin's sword with spring leaves, Sentinel's server watchtower. Archer: Ranger's two-orbit atom, Skirmisher's winged phone, Herald's horn with a code pennant. Mage: Enchanter's rune pipeline, Summoner's container crate, Seer's floating eye.

Each job also has an accent colour. It is the prop's main colour and also trims the outfit: the Warrior's tabard flap, the Archer's tunic yoke and the Mage's robe bands. The colours are the same in both themes. Gear still draws over the trim, and the Adventurer is unchanged.

Each prop has its own short action (a shield glint, blinking status lights, electrons moving along their orbits, a lid lifting, an eye blinking). It plays under the same rules as before: only when motion is allowed, and it stops after a few seconds.

Saved looks now use the job name, such as `mage/seer`. A look saved by an earlier version, such as `mage/hawk`, resets once to the GitHub pick.
